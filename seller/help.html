Seller Help Center

Getting Started

Welcome to the SHG Marketplace Seller Portal! Here’s how you can manage your products and sales:

  • Add New Products: Go to "Add Product" section, fill in name, description, price, image, and initial stock.
  • Update Stock: Navigate to your product list and update the quantity for each product.
  • Remove Products: Click the "Remove" button next to the product you want to delete.
  • Track Orders: Once buyers checkout, you can view orders in your dashboard (future feature).

Tips for Success

  • Use high-quality images to showcase your products.
  • Provide clear and detailed descriptions.
  • Keep stock levels updated regularly.
  • Engage with buyers for better ratings and repeat sales.
