About SHG Marketplace Seller Portal

Our Mission

SHG Marketplace is designed to empower Self Help Groups (SHGs) and local artisans by providing a digital platform to sell their products directly to buyers. Our seller portal helps you manage your products, stock, and reach a wider audience.

Benefits for Sellers

  • Easy product management: Add, update, or remove your products.
  • Reach more customers directly without intermediaries.
  • Increase sales and promote local craftsmanship.
  • Access simple dashboard for tracking inventory and orders.

Future Plans & Innovations

  • Order tracking and automated notifications.
  • Seller analytics and performance dashboard.
  • Integrated payment gateway for smooth transactions.
  • Mobile-friendly interface and app support.
