About Us

Problems We Noticed

  • SHG (Self Help Group) products usually don’t get proper online visibility.
  • Buyers face difficulty in finding authentic handmade & organic products.
  • Lack of direct platform where sellers (SHGs) can list and manage their products easily.
  • Most marketplaces charge high commissions, reducing SHG profit margins.

Our Solutions

  • A simple website where buyers can directly explore SHG products.
  • Easy product management (add/update/remove) for sellers.
  • No complex setup — everything is accessible with basic login (demo version).
  • Minimal/no commission model to support local SHGs directly.

Future Plans & Innovations

  • Integration of digital payments (UPI/Wallet) for direct transactions.
  • Adding product reviews & ratings for trust building.
  • Mobile-friendly responsive app for SHGs in rural areas.
  • AI-based product recommendations for buyers.
  • Logistics tie-up to help SHGs deliver across India smoothly.
